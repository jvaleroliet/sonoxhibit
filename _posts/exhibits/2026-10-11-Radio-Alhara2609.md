Last month on Radio Alhara , Gabriel Sabot and I hosted a radio program that pays tribute to the popular anti-fascist songs that emerged from the turmoil of the Spanish Civil War and the legacy they left both within and outside of the Spanish territory after 1939.

This year (2026) marks the 90th anniversary of the fascist military coup that triggered the onslaught of the Spanish Civil War, and we wanted to take this opportunity to pay a little homage and remember the countless people who gave their lives fighting for freedom, especially in a time when fascism is gaining wider legitimacy.

You will listen to resistance chants, clandestine recordings during the dictatorship period, and exile songs.
