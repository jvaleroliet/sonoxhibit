*ACUSTIKAS* is an ongoing project documenting Vallekas's soundscape.

Vallekas is a district in southeastern Madrid, formed by Puente de Vallecas and Villa de Vallecas. Historically working-class and antifascist, it grew rapidly in the 1950s due to rural migration, workers drawn by industry and construction jobs. 

Over the decades, the neighborhood has been a center of social movements, labor organizing, housing and community activism, resisting urban displacement and defending local identity. 

The area is defined by busy streets, overpasses, and public spaces, with constant traffic noise, street sounds, music, and daily activity shaping its urban soundscape.
