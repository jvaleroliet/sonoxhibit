In 2020, Adrián Lattes and I created *radio klein*, a community radio station that broadcast from Madrid on weekends.

We set up the station on a server recycling an old computer we had at home.

Many friends and acquaintances joined in, either by hosting their own shows or by tuning in as listeners. 

You can access the [radio archive here](https://haztecaso.com/radio-old/archivo/audios/).
