Sono Mayrit is a radio producer and sonic researcher focused on the interconnections of the Mediterranean region. His collaborative projects explore the parallels between tradition, sonic experimentation and socio-cultural narratives.

Over the past six years he has worked with artists, radio stations and cultural institutions, curating and producing radio shows, live performances, listening sessions, discussion panels and more.
