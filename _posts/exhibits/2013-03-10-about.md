Juan is a mathematician, audio developer, musician, sound artist and radio producer, focused on the interconnections of the Mediterranean region. 

Under the project _sonomayrit_, he explores the parallels between tradition, sonic experimentation and socio-cultural narratives.

Over the past seven years he has worked with artists, radio stations and cultural institutions, curating and producing radio shows, live performances, listening sessions, discussion panels and more.
