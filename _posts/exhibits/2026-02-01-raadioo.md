*raadioo* is a simple radio player for experimental community radios. It emulates the user experience of a traditional radio receiver.

I created it tired of having to change between websites to listen to the different stations, most of which are powered by very heavy websites that consume resources, track your data (and sell it), and distract you. 

Visit it here:

[https://raadioo.org](https://raadioo.org)

You can also access to the source code and create your own version [here](https://github.com/jvaleroliet/raadioo). I will be happy to include it as a subdomain.
