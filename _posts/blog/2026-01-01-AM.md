A few days after Radio Nacional de España unexpectedly switched off its AM broadcasts on December 30, 2025 after 88 years of emission, I scanned this band from one of the highest points in Madrid. 

<!--more-->

The spectrum is fairly empty, but I picked up a frequency from North Africa.

It was so cold and suddenly we saw a fire starting on one building down in the Albufera. You can hear the firefighters in the background getting to the fire.
