Welcome to your new blog feed! This is a minimal, article-based feed integrated into your sonoxhibit site.

## Key Features

- **Chronological feed** - Entries display newest first
- **Minimal aesthetic** - Matches your site's design while being clean and article-focused
- **Easy to use** - Just drop markdown files in `_posts/blog/`
- **Integrated navigation** - Blog appears in your site's left sidebar under a "Blog" section

## Quick Start

1. Create a new markdown file in `_posts/blog/`
2. Name it with the date: `YYYY-MM-DD-title.md`
3. Add front matter with `category: blog` and `date`
4. Write your content
5. Push to GitHub and Jekyll will build it automatically

## What's Different from Your Exhibits

- **Blog entries** are focused on chronological log entries
- **Shorter format** - Works well for quick updates, thoughts, documentation
- **Text-forward** - Though images and media are fully supported
- **Separate section** - Keeps your exhibits and blog distinct

Feel free to customize the styling in `screen.css` under the "BLOG FEED STYLES" section if you want to adjust colors, spacing, or typography.
